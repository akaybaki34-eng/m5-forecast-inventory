Forecast to Inventory

Ten demand-forecasting models scored twice: on forecast error, and on what their forecasts cost when they drive the same periodic-review, lost-sales stock policy. The most accurate model is not the cheapest one, and the safety-stock rule moves cost more than the model choice.

Accuracy against cost

Each dot is a model. Left is more accurate, lower is cheaper. Outlined dots sit on the Pareto front: no other model beats them on both.

Safety-stock rule × forecast model

196-day simulation with stock carried between windows. Shading ranks each cell within the whole matrix; the best cell per column is marked.

The textbook rule assumes independent daily errors. Recursive forecast errors are autocorrelated, so the 12-day error spread is about 1.5× what σ·√H predicts and the 95 % target is missed. The empirical rule uses the 95th percentile of past 12-day cumulative errors instead.
